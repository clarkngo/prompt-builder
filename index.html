How it works

These tools don’t talk to an AI for you. They help you write a better brief — then you paste it into the assistant of your choice.

1

Shape the request

Pick the task type, audience, constraints, and quality bars that matter for your context.

2

Copy the prompt

Generate a structured prompt, copy it, and open it in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or another LLM.

3

Review before use

AI drafts still need your subject-matter judgment, institutional voice, and accuracy check.
